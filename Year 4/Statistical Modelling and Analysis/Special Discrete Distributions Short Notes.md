# Bernoulli
$Bernoulli(p)$ is the chance that an event with probability $p$ occurs.
$p_X(1)=$ chance that the event occurred whereas
$p_X(0)=$ chance that the event didn't occur.
$\mu = \mathbb{E}(X)=p$ 
# Uniform
Chance for any one outcome to occur in $k$ outcomes
$p_X(x)=\frac{1}{6}$ for $x=1,2,...,6$ 
$\mu=\mathbb{E}(X)=\frac{k+1}{2}$ 
# Binomial
$Bin(n,p)$ is the number of successes in a sequence of $n$ Bernoulli trials with probability $p$.
$p_X(x)=\left(n\atop x\right)p^x\times q^{n-x},\ x=0,1,...,n$ 
## Poisson Random Variables
$X\sim Poi(\lambda)$ 
Number of events which occur in a unit of time or space where events occur at random one after another with rate $\lambda$.
$p_X(x)=\frac{e^{\lambda}\lambda^x}{x!}$  
$\mu = \lambda$
